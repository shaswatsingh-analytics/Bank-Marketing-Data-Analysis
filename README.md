## 📌 About The Project
Welcome to my data analysis portfolio! In this project, I took on the role of a Data Analyst to solve a real-world business problem for a retail bank. 

Banks often spend a huge amount of money and time calling thousands of customers to sell term deposits. But is cold-calling everyone the best strategy? I analyzed over 41,000 customer records to find out exactly *who* subscribes, *why* they subscribe, and how the bank can stop wasting money on untargeted marketing.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights (What the data told us)
Instead of just showing code, here is what I actually found from the data:

1. **Pristine Data is the Foundation:** I audited the dataset of 41,176 records. By ensuring there were zero missing values and removing exact duplicates, I made sure all our business decisions are based on accurate data.
2. **Mass Marketing is Failing:** A massive **89%** of the contacted customers said "No" to the term deposit. This proves that calling people randomly is highly inefficient and causes a huge drain on the bank's operational budget.
3. **The Economy Matters More Than The Call:** Through a correlation heatmap, I discovered that customers are heavily influenced by macroeconomic indicators (like the 3-month Euribor interest rate and employment variation rates). People invest based on the economy, not just because they received a phone call.

---

## 💡 Final Recommendation & Next Steps
**Stop mass calling and start predicting.** 
The bank should shift from a broad "call-everyone" campaign to a targeted strategy. The immediate next step is to use this clean dataset to train an **Intelligent Machine Learning Model** (like Random Forest or Logistic Regression). By predicting which specific customers have a high probability of saying "Yes" based on their economic and personal profiles, the bank can cut marketing costs drastically while increasing actual conversions.
